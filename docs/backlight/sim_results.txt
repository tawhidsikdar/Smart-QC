====================================================

light-box walls as mirrors with reflectance R (R=0 = black / no walls).
Diffuser smoothing and light recycling are NOT included, so real results should be
equal or better.

Reading the results
- LED spots disappear once the LED spacing P is at most ~0.8 x the distance H (ripple < 0.6 %).
- Black or missing walls lose ~half the brightness at the edges; white walls keep 91-97 %.
- Chosen: H = 53.5 mm, 120 LEDs, white light box.
