- STEP exports of parts and assembly
- PDF drawings
- DXF files for cutting
- bom.csv (bill of materials)

## What does NOT go here
- Native CAD files (keep them in Google Drive: <paste Drive folder link>)
- Files bigger than 50 MB

## Rules
- Only one person edits a part at a time. Say it in the group chat first.
- Upload a new export whenever a part design is final.
